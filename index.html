The Firm

A modern practice without losing the human relationship.

Oakbridge is a fictional demonstration firm created to show how a legal practice can combine a professional public website with a secure digital client experience.

The final system can be branded around the real firm's practitioners, credentials, locations, services and preferred client workflow.
